1Agua Vecina — Find Water, Coordinate, mobile
Assumptions and decisions
Real map (OpenStreetMap data, CARTO light tiles) centered on San Juan–Carolina, desaturated so the markers carry the color. Instrument Sans for the interface; DM Serif Display only in the wordmark and the main question. The resident’s location is shown as an ~550 m "approximate area", never an address. One warm background tone (#FAF7F2) and 10–14 px radii throughout. All three screens are interactive: markers select, "Request delivery" confirms, and the Coordinate state switches with the buttons under its label.
